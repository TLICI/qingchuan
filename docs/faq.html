ABOUT WEB INDEXING

What is Web Indexing?

Web Indexing is an optional feature powered by the Bright SDK, provided by Bright Data. When you turn it on, the app uses your device's idle resources and internet connection to access publicly available web data — pages anyone can open without a login or password.

This is an alternative to advertising. Instead of showing ads, it helps support the app's development.

Who is Bright Data?

Bright Data is the company behind the Bright SDK. It is an established provider of public web data services, used by universities, banks, retailers and research organisations.

What data does it access?

Only publicly available web pages. It does not access your personal files, photos, messages, contacts, browsing history or any other private content on your device. Bright Data does not collect, store or sell your personal information, and the feature is compliant with GDPR and CCPA.

Is it on by default?

No. Web Indexing is off until you explicitly agree. A full consent screen from Bright Data is shown before anything starts, and nothing runs unless you accept it. Participation is entirely voluntary.

Will it slow down my phone or drain the battery?

It is built to run quietly in the background and to stay within limits set by the operating system. It does not use your storage for downloaded pages — data is forwarded without being kept on the device. It does not mine cryptocurrency and does not send email.

CHANGING YOUR CHOICE

How do I turn Web Indexing off?

Open Settings → Web Indexing and switch it off. The same screen also has a Turn off Web Indexing button. Either one revokes your consent immediately and stops the feature — you do not need to contact us and there is no waiting period.

Can I turn it back on later?

Yes, at any time, from the same settings screen. You can switch it on and off as often as you like — there is no limit and no penalty.

Does turning it off affect the app?

The app keeps working normally. You can still draw, collect lights and use the whole landscape. Some extras — the season selector, firefly lights, custom scene and free arrangement — are tied to participation and become locked again. Turning it back on restores them right away.

Where can I read more?

Bright Data's own end-user FAQ: https://brightdata.com/sdk/information

Privacy policy: https://brightdata.com/legal/sdk-privacy

常见问题（中文）

Web Indexing 是什么？

Web Indexing 是由 Bright Data 提供的可选功能。开启后，应用会使用设备的空闲资源 与网络连接访问公开可得的网页数据——即无需账号密码、任何人都能打开的内容。它是一种替代广告的 支持方式，不展示广告。

会不会影响手机性能？

它被设计为在后台安静运行，并遵守系统给出的资源限制；下载的网页不会保存在本机， 也不占用存储空间，不挖矿、不发邮件。

默认是开启的吗？

不是。必须由你主动同意，同意前会显示 Bright Data 的完整授权说明。 参与完全自愿。

怎么关闭？关闭后有什么影响？

在「设置 → Web Indexing」把开关拨到关闭，或点同页面的「关闭 Web Indexing」按钮， 授权会立即撤销，功能随即停止。关闭后应用照常使用，只是四季切换、流萤光点、自定义布景与自由 摆放会重新上锁；再次开启即可立刻恢复。

可以反复开关吗？

可以，次数不限，也没有任何等待期或惩罚。
