Privacy Policy

Qingchuan (晴川) · com.qingchuan.connect

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 28 日

In short: Qingchuan collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cloud sync. Everything you create stays on your device. The only optional third-party component is Bright Data's Bright SDK, which is off unless you explicitly turn it on, and can be turned off again at any time in Settings.

1. WHO WE ARE

Qingchuan is an offline landscape app for Android, published by an independent developer (GitHub account TLICI). This policy covers the app itself and the optional Web Indexing feature it integrates.

Contact for any privacy question: tgosec@163.com

2. DATA WE COLLECT OURSELVES

None. The app has no account system, no sign-in, no server of its own, and no network calls of its own. We do not collect, transmit or sell personal information, and we have no way to identify individual users.

Specifically, the app does not access or collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or contacts
  • your location, or any device identifier such as IMEI, MAC address or advertising ID
  • your photos, files, camera, microphone or clipboard
  • usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioural tracking of any kind

3. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Your settings are saved locally in Android's private app storage and never leave the device. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. THIRD PARTY: BRIGHT DATA (BRIGHT SDK)

The app integrates Bright SDK, provided by Bright Data Ltd. This section describes exactly what that means.

4.1 What Web Indexing is

Web Indexing is an optional feature that lets the app support itself without showing ads. When — and only when — you explicitly opt in, the Bright SDK uses your device's idle resources and internet connection to access publicly available web data, meaning pages anyone can open without a login or password. Downloaded pages are not stored on your device.

4.2 It is off by default

Web Indexing is disabled by default. A full consent screen from Bright Data is shown before anything starts, and nothing runs unless you accept it. Declining, or never opening the screen, leaves the feature completely inactive.

4.3 What Bright Data does and does not do

Bright Data states that it does not collect, store or sell personal information, and that the feature is compliant with the GDPR and the CCPA. The SDK does not access your personal files, photos, messages, contacts or browsing history. It does not mine cryptocurrency and does not send email.

4.4 How to withdraw your consent

Open Settings → Web Indexing and switch it off, or tap Turn off Web Indexing on the same screen. Consent is revoked immediately and the SDK service stops. You can opt in and out as many times as you like; there is no limit and no penalty. Some in-app extras (season selector, firefly lights, custom scene, free arrangement) are tied to participation and become locked again after opt-out — the base app keeps working normally.

4.5 More information

  • Bright SDK end-user FAQ: brightdata.com/sdk/information
  • Bright Data SDK privacy policy: brightdata.com/legal/sdk-privacy

5. PERMISSIONS

The app declares only two permissions: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. Both are used solely by the Bright SDK when Web Indexing is enabled — the second one only to tell whether a network is available. No runtime permission is requested, and when Web Indexing is off the app makes no network connections at all.

6. CHILDREN

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete on your behalf. To erase everything the app stores, uninstall it or use Android's Clear data. To stop the Bright SDK, turn Web Indexing off in Settings.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "last updated" date. Material changes affecting the Bright SDK integration will also be noted in the app.
